Top Case with fixture holes

---

The top case of the T-Minus watch case is a very thin piece, so holding it in a standard industrial sized CNC machine can prove to be challenging. To this end a modification of the CAD was made to allow 2 M4 Bolts to be passed through and tightened to a workholding piece machined out of Poliurethane with a fitting to the internal part of the case.
